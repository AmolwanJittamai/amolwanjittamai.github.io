Research

Behavioral questions at the intersection of supply chains and sustainability.

My research interests center on supply chain transparency, sustainable supply chain management, and behavioral operations.

01

Supply chain transparency

How disclosure — including disclosure of shortcomings — changes consumer perceptions, trust, and product valuation.

02

Sustainable operations

How organizations and managers prioritize social and environmental sustainability practices across global supply chains.

03

Behavioral operations + AI

How human decision-makers interact with AI in sustainability decisions, and how those collaborations influence outcomes.
